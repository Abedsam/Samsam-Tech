Contact Form

When you send us an inquiry via the contact form, we store the data you provide (first name, last name, email address, optionally company and phone number, subject and your message) in order to process your inquiry and for any follow-up questions. Processing is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) or on taking steps prior to entering into a contract at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

We use the Web3Forms service to technically transmit the form. Your input is transmitted to Web3Forms' servers and forwarded to us by email from there. Data from the contact form remains with us until you ask us to delete it, withdraw your consent, or the purpose for storing it no longer applies.

Cookies
